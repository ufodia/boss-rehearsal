Initial local video-authoring and practice application:

- Separate skips, interrupted-attempt retry and structured session results.
- Portable lessons and embedded-video standalone trainers.
- Original synthetic demo and its tracked generator.
- Source/browser checks and English documentation.

Metrics describe the authored drill. Codec/controller compatibility and in-game transfer are separate verification questions.
